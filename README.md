# 🌟 Lipishilpo Pro (লিপিশিল্প প্রো) - Book Get-Up Studio, AI Editorial & Publication Powerhouse

**Lipishilpo Pro** is the premium companion add-on for the [Lipishilpo](https://github.com/itsmanzur/lipishilpo) WordPress plugin. It transforms WordPress into an OS-grade **Book Get-Up Studio**, complete with 4-pillar layout engineering, dynamic page numbering, 5 pre-styled Table of Contents presets, paper GSM spine calculators, full wrap cover artboards, EAN-13 ISBN barcodes, 1-click Preflight Quality Inspector, realistic 2-page spread & 3D mockup live preview, deep OpenAI GPT-4o literary analysis, and press-ready PDF / EPUB 3.0 / Word (.docx) exports.

---

## 🚀 Pro Features & Architecture

### 1. 📚 4-Pillar Book Get-Up Studio (বুক গেট-আপ স্টুডিও)
- **Pillar 1: Layout & Typography:** Popular Bengali trim sizes (A5, 1/8 Demy, Crown Octavo, Royal, US Trade 6×9, Custom), inside/outside margins, binding gutter calculations, premium Bengali fonts (Kalpurush, SolaimanLipi, Shurjo), and leading/tracking control.
- **Pillar 2: Pages & Dynamic TOC:**
  - **Dynamic Page Numbering:** 1-click position switching (Bottom Center, Bottom Alternating Outside, Top Header), formatting (Bengali numerals `১, ২, ৩`, decorative `— ১২ —`, bullets `• ১২ •`, Roman `i, ii, iii`), and smart front matter skipping.
  - **5 Predefined TOC Styles:** Classic Dotted Leader, Minimalist Modern, Elegant Roman, Romantic Decorative, and Boxed Grid.
  - **Chapter Openers & Motifs:** Multi-line Drop Caps, Victorian floral and geometric ornamental dividers.
- **Pillar 3: Cover & Spine Engineering:**
  - **Paper GSM & Spine Calculator:** Automatic spine millimeter thickness calculation based on paper weight (65 GSM Newsprint, 70 GSM Offset, 80 GSM, 100 GSM Art Paper), page count, and binding type (Paperback vs Hardcover Board).
  - **Full Cover Wrap:** Seamlessly merges Front Cover + Spine + Back Cover + 5mm Bleed into a single print-ready canvas.
  - **Vector EAN-13 Barcode & Price Tag:** Automated back cover ISBN barcode generator with localized price tags.
- **Pillar 4: Preflight Quality Check & Press-Ready Exports:**
  - **1-Click Preflight Inspector:** Instant scanning for safe zone violations (3mm cutting edge safety), bleed errors, text overflow, font embeddings, and page signature imposition.
  - **Multi-Format Export Engine:** Press-ready PDF with crop marks, standard EPUB 3.0, and Microsoft Word (.docx). Kindle/tablet/mobile are preview modes in the studio, not separate MOBI files.

### 2. 🖥️ Interactive Live Preview & 3D Mockup Engine
- **Realistic 2-Page Spread:** Side-by-side even/odd page layout with page-turn curves and binding shadows.
- **Safe Zone & Trim Line Overlay:** Toggleable graphic overlay showing trim lines, 3mm safe areas, and 5mm bleeds.
- **Realtime 3D Book Mockup:** Interactive 3D hardcover and paperback book preview.
- **Multi-Device Simulation:** Print paper simulation, Kindle E-Reader, Tablet, and Mobile views.
- **Section & Chapter Browser:** One-click jumping between Front Matter (Title, Copyright, Dedication), Table of Contents, Chapters, and Back Matter.

### 3. 🤖 Deep AI Editorial Companion & TTS Audio Proofreader
- **OpenAI GPT-4o Editorial Engine:** Proofreading, literary phrasing, scene-by-scene tension curve analysis, and whole-book plot/character continuity tracking.
- **Text-to-Speech (TTS) Audio Proofreading:** Multi-speed neural playback with synchronized real-time sentence focus cursors.

## 📋 Requirements
- **WordPress:** 6.0 or higher.
- **Parent Plugin:** [Lipishilpo (Free Version)](https://github.com/itsmanzur/lipishilpo) installed and active.
- **PHP:** 7.4 or higher.

---

## 💻 Installation & Setup

1. Upload the `lipishilpo-pro` folder to your WordPress `/wp-content/plugins/` directory.
2. Ensure the base `lipishilpo` plugin is installed and activated.
3. Activate **Lipishilpo Pro** from the WordPress Plugins screen.
4. Navigate to **Lipishilpo > Settings** in WordPress admin to configure your OpenAI API Key and Pro License Key.
5. Launch the **LipiShilpo Editor** or click **Open Book Studio** to start formatting and publishing books.
